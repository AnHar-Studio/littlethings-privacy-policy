Privacy Policy

Little Things

1. Introduction

AnHar Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Little Things mobile application (“App”) handles information when you use our services.

Little Things is designed to help users preserve memories, manage important dates, save favorites, organize gift ideas, and create reminders for meaningful moments.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Little Things may allow you to create or store information such as:

  • Personal memories and notes
  • Photos and images you choose
  • Partner information and favorite items
  • Important dates, anniversaries, and reminders
  • Preferences and app settings

When information is stored locally on your device, we do not receive or access it remotely.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location unless a feature or third-party service explicitly requires it.

3. Photos and Media

The App may request access to photos or media when you choose to add images to your memories or other features.

  • Access is used to support the functionality you request.
  • We do not independently upload your selected photos to our servers.
  • Photos remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them through another application or service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information created within the App may be stored locally on your device.

We do not operate our own servers to store personal memories or photos under the local-storage-only configuration.

We take reasonable measures to protect the App and its stored information. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any backups.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services in future versions, including analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or cloud services.

Such services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

If third-party services are integrated into the App, this Privacy Policy will be updated to identify the relevant services and explain their data practices.

6. Data Sharing

Under the local-storage-only configuration:

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your memories or photos with our own servers.
  • We do not disclose personal information to third parties unless you initiate sharing, it is required by law, or the App's functionality explicitly involves a third-party service.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it.

You can remove information by deleting individual entries within the App, where supported, or by uninstalling the App.

Please note that uninstalling the App may permanently delete locally stored information unless it has been backed up separately.

8. Children's Privacy

Little Things is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a service associated with the App, please contact us so that we can investigate.

9. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, Little Things may request permissions such as:

  • Photos or media access
  • Notifications for reminders
  • Biometric authentication, if enabled
  • Other permissions necessary for specific features

Permissions are used only to support the relevant functionality.

Biometric authentication is handled by your device's operating system. The App does not receive your fingerprint or facial biometric data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be published at the Privacy Policy URL associated with the App. The effective date will be updated accordingly.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about your personal data.

For information stored locally, you can generally manage or delete it directly within the App or on your device.
